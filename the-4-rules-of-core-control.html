Why core control matters

What is core control?
Core control is how your body coordinates tension, breath and movement. A controlled core can let go, breathe, adjust its tension and move in parts.
Why does core control matter?
Your core is the foundation of everything you do in training. When your control is missing, every exercise multiplies the strain. When it's there, everything starts working together.
What happens when my core control is missing?
You brace harder and still feel weak. Your neck, jaw and lower back do the work your deep core should be doing, and your lifts feel forced.
How do I know if I have a core control problem?
You feel your neck or lower back in your core work. You hold your breath to lift. You brace the same way for a plank as for a heavy deadlift. Your core feels weak, stiff or switched off.
Why haven't core workouts fixed this?
Most core workouts add more tension on top of tension. If your core can't let go, breathe or adjust, more exercises only make it more tired.
