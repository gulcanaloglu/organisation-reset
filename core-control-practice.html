Core control comes before more strength.

Most people who train try to fix their core one exercise at a time: more planks, more dead bugs, another Pilates class, then heavier lifts. But if the control underneath all of it is missing, every new exercise multiplies the strain.

Imagine knowing exactly what your core needs before every workout: where you're starting from, how each of the 4 Rules feels in your body, the one rule to start with, and the exact exercises you'll do.

That's the Core Control Practice. It makes the decisions most people who train skip: what to work on first, how long to spend on it, and how to check that it worked.

Without it, you're fixing your core one exercise at a time and hoping it adds up. With it, every workout finally starts from the same place.
